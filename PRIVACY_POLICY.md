# Privacy Policy for DopaQueue

**Last Updated:** July 10, 2026  

Thank you for choosing **DopaQueue** ("we," "our," or "us"). Your privacy and data security are of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how DopaQueue collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you install and use the DopaQueue browser extension.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

DopaQueue is designed with a **privacy-first, user-controlled** architecture. Depending on how you configure the extension, we process the following categories of information:

### A. Information Processed Locally on Your Device
- **Watchlist & Consumption Queue:** Web page titles, URLs, timestamps, tags, and urgency ratings that you save or enqueue.
- **Consumption Analytics:** Local statistics regarding your viewing patterns, gamification progress (DopaScore, levels, streaks), and notes.
- **Extension Preferences:** User settings such as daily budget thresholds, theme preferences, and UI toggles.

### B. Cloud Sync Information (Optional / User-Initiated)
If you choose to sign in with your Google Account to sync your data across devices via cloud storage (Supabase):
- **Authentication Data:** Your email address, display name, and profile picture provided via Google OAuth.
- **Synced Queue Data:** Your watchlist items, notes, gamification state, and preferences encrypted/stored securely in your user-isolated cloud database rows.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the processed data **solely** to provide and improve DopaQueue's core functionality:
1. **Productivity & Dopamine Analysis:** Categorizing links, calculating dopamine budgets, tracking watch times, and generating insights to help you manage digital consumption.
2. **Cross-Device Synchronization:** Enabling you to access your saved watchlist and notes across multiple browsers or devices when logged in.
3. **Gamification & Habit Tracking:** Maintaining your streak, XP, and levels to reward healthy media consumption habits.

---

## 3. Explicit User Consent for Content Analysis

By installing and using DopaQueue, **you explicitly consent** to allowing the extension to inspect active tab metadata (URLs, page titles, and timestamps) on supported platforms (such as YouTube or web articles) for the purpose of analyzing content duration, categorizing media, and managing your dopamine budget.

- **No Unauthorized Scraping:** DopaQueue only analyzes pages you actively browse or save.
- **No Advertising or Data Selling:** We **never** sell, rent, or trade your personal data, browsing history, or analytics to advertisers, data brokers, or any third parties.

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- **Local Storage:** By default, your data resides entirely on your local machine using standard browser storage APIs (`chrome.storage.local`).
- **Cloud Storage:** If you enable Cloud Sync, your data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS channels to secure cloud infrastructure powered by Supabase. Access to cloud data is protected by Row Level Security (RLS) policies tied exclusively to your authenticated user account.

---

While we implement industry-standard encryption, authentication, and security protocols to safeguard your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. 

By using DopaQueue, you acknowledge and agree that:
- You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and device security.
- **The developer(s) and publisher(s) of DopaQueue shall not be held liable or responsible for any unauthorized access, security breaches, or data leaks** resulting from device compromise, third-party service vulnerabilities (including Google OAuth or Supabase cloud infrastructure), or user misconfiguration.

---

## 6. Your Rights & Control

You retain full control over your data at all times:
- **Export Data:** You can export your entire queue, notes, and analytics to Markdown, CSV, or JSON directly from the DopaQueue Settings page.
- **Delete Local Data:** You can clear all locally stored extension data at any time via your browser extension settings or within the app.
- **Account Deletion / Sign Out:** Signing out stops all cloud synchronization immediately.
